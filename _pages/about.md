I trained in theory and computation during my PhD at the University of Virginia, building closed-form models of multi-physics fields and checking them numerically. At Tesla I model battery cells and other engineering systems, and apply reinforcement learning to automate and scale them. My current question is whether AI can reason with real physics and real engineering artifacts; [BenchCAD](/projects/1_project/), a benchmark for writing the CAD programs behind industrial parts, is my main test of it.

Open to conversations about AI for hardware and physics-based ML.
