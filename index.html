    BenchCAD: A Comprehensive, Industry-Standard Benchmark for Programmatic CAD
    Haozhe Zhang†, Kaichen Liu, Miaomiao Chen, Lei Li, Shaojie Yang, Cheng Peng, and Hanjie Chen†
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.10865 2026
    † Corresponding authors
    ★ Reported in Anthropic system cards and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 launch
    Abs URL Project

    We present BenchCAD, a comprehensive industry-standard benchmark for evaluating programmatic CAD code generation. The benchmark includes over 17,000 execution-verified CadQuery programs spanning 106 industrial part families (e.g., gears, springs, drills) and assesses models across visual question answering, code analysis, image-to-code conversion, and code editing. Our results show that current models often recover coarse outer geometry but fail to produce faithful parametric CAD programs, with common failure modes including missing structural details and oversimplified operations; generalization to unseen part families remains limited even after fine-tuning.
